# Cell biology

The cell is the basic unit of life. Mitochondria produce most of the cell's ATP through oxidative phosphorylation.

## Flashcards #flashcards/science/biology
What organelle produces most of a cell's ATP?::The **mitochondrion**, via oxidative phosphorylation.
What is the function of ribosomes?::They translate mRNA into proteins.
What are the four nucleotide bases of DNA?::Adenine, thymine, guanine and cytosine (A, T, G, C).
Where does glycolysis take place?::In the cytoplasm.
What does the Golgi apparatus do?::Modifies, sorts and packages proteins for secretion or delivery.
What is osmosis?::Diffusion of water across a semipermeable membrane, from low to high solute concentration.
Which molecule carries amino acids to the ribosome?::Transfer RNA (tRNA).
