# Physics

## Mechanics #flashcards/science/physics
Newton's second law?::$F = ma$
Kinetic energy of a mass $m$ moving at speed $v$?::$E_k = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$
Period of a simple pendulum of length $L$?::$T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$
What is the unit of power?::The watt: $1\,\text{W} = 1\,\text{J/s}$
Escape velocity from a body of mass $M$, radius $r$?::$v_e = \sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}$

## Waves #flashcards/science/physics
Relation between speed, frequency and wavelength?::$v = f\lambda$
What is the Doppler effect?::A change in observed frequency when source and observer move relative to each other.
